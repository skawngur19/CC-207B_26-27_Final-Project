CLINCH STUDY HUB - LOCAL WEBSITE DEMO

1. Right-click Clinch-Local-Demo.zip and choose Extract All.
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click index.html.
3. Choose Customer, Employee, or Owner. Use 1234 in both email and password.

No server, Node.js, Flutter, installation, or Internet connection is needed.
Use Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome with JavaScript enabled.

The demo includes reservations, recruitment, cash checkout, and owner staff controls.
Changes stay in this browser on this device. Everyone testing has separate demo records.
All accounts, bookings and receipts are test data. Use sample information only.

Keep the files and the assets folder together. Open index.html after extraction,
rather than opening it inside the ZIP.
